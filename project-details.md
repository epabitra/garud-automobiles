1. Executive Summary

The available evidence suggests that Garud Automobiles is a small local automotive/EV showroom in Bijipur, Brahmapur (Berhampur), Odisha, with a particularly strong visual association with Skyy Rider Electric.

The supplied Google profile identifies it as a showroom, while an indexed Justdial result associates Garud Automobiles with an automobile horn/car horn product listing and gives it a separate 4.8/5 rating from 15 ratings.
